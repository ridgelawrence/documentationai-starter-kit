## Change categories

| Tag | Meaning | When to use |
|-----|---------|-------------|
| feature | New content or documentation capability | You added a page, section, or workflow |
| improvement | Better clarity or structure | You refined wording, layout, or navigation |
| bugfix | Correction to inaccurate content | You fixed a broken link or unclear instruction |
| breaking | A change that alters previous guidance | You changed a process that readers must follow differently |
