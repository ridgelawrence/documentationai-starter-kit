## Quickstart

WRLD Tech is the help center and documentation hub for every WRLD division. Use this guide to sign in, choose the right area, and find the first resources you need without bouncing between products.

<Callout kind="info">

Before you begin, make sure you can access the WRLD dashboard, your support email, and the division you plan to use first.

</Callout>

## Choose your starting path

WRLD Tech supports multiple divisions, so the first task is deciding where to begin. If you are working with hosting, start with WRLD.host. If you are managing brand assets or creative work, start with WRLD.design. If your focus is support workflows, start with WRLD.Support.

## First visit workflow

Follow these steps the first time you open the documentation hub.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to the portal" icon="lock">
    
Open the WRLD dashboard and confirm that your account has access to the division you need. If your workspace is still being set up, ask your team lead to verify permissions before you continue.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the documentation index" icon="book-open">
    
Start with the documentation index to discover available pages. Use it as your map before searching for a specific article or workflow.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the right division" icon="arrow-right">
    
Choose the division that matches your task. This keeps your navigation focused and helps you avoid duplicate or outdated instructions.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save useful pages" icon="check-circle">
    
Bookmark the pages you use often, such as onboarding, account access, or support submission. That gives you a faster starting point next time.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to do next

After you complete your first visit, move into the page that matches your role. If you are still exploring, start with the overview for the division you use most often. Then use search, bookmarks, and the index page to build your own workflow.

<Expandable title="How do I know which page is current?" default-open="false">

Look for the most recent guidance on the division overview page, and compare it with the documentation index. When in doubt, use the index first and then drill into the relevant section.

</Expandable>

<Expandable title="What if I do not see the page I need?" default-open="false">

Check whether the page lives under a different division. If you still cannot find it, open a support ticket or email `helpdesk@wrld.tech` with the page name and what you are trying to do.

</Expandable>
