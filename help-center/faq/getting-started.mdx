## Getting Started

1. Sign up at `https://dashboard.wrld.tech`.
2. Verify your email address.
3. Log in with your credentials.
4. Create a new WRLD project from the dashboard.
5. Add your team (optional).

## Quick Access

Go to your WRLD dashboard after login to view documentation, API keys, and settings.

Need help? Contact support anytime at `support@wrld.tech`.
