---
title: Trouble Logging In
description: Steps to fix WRLD login issues.
---

## Symptoms
- Incorrect email or password error.
- "Unable to log in" message.

## Common Fixes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reset Password">
    ````
    Use the "Forgot password" link on the login page.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Email Verified">
    ````
    Ensure you have verified your email (check inbox).
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear Cookies">
    ````
    Clear your browser cookies and cache, then try again.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
