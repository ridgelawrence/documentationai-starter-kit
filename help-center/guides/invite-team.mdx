---

## Team Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Project Settings">
    ````
    From the WRLD dashboard, select your project and click "Settings".
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Members">
    ````
    Enter your team member's email addresses in the "Invite" field and send invites.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign Roles">
    ````
    Assign each member a role (Admin, Editor, Viewer) as needed.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>

Tip: Team members will receive an invite link via email to join your WRLD project.
